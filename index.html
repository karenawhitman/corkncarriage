Photo truck · booking now

Park the Chevy. The camera does the rest.

The cornerstone of Cork & Carriage is the 1950 Chevrolet 3600 Advance Design — a ¾-ton truck with a chrome grille, rented as a photography backdrop. You choose the photographer and a location in our service area. We place the truck and stay out of the frame.

Spring

Bloom season on the rivers. Dogwoods, azaleas, and engagement or bridal portraits on lawns from Gloucester to Irvington.

Summer

Golden hour on the Rappahannock and Piankatank. Warm color, long light, and a working vintage truck against field and water.

Fall

Harvest color and the Urbanna Oyster Festival. Maple, marsh, and the green truck against foliage and yacht-club lawns.

Winter

Wreaths on the grille, string lights, and holiday cards. Quiet winter light reflecting off a truck that refuses to quit.

Engagements & portraits

Couples across the service area. The 3600 is the backdrop — chrome, color, and a piece of history in the frame.

Wedding-day 3600

Portraits with the Chevy on the wedding-day lawn. The truck is for photographs; the rest of the day stays yours.

Family & holiday cards

Holiday cards and family sessions with wreaths, pumpkins, or lights on a real 1950 truck — not a studio backdrop.

Styled & brand

Photographers and small brands who need a period truck. Image-use terms are written into the contract.

3600 Uncorked · Spring 2027

The bar arrives. The pour is yours.

3600 Uncorked is a 1963 Yellowstone canned-ham trailer finished as a dry-hire mobile bar and towed by the same 3600. It is built for river weddings and Saturday events in oyster country. Service begins Spring 2027. Join the waitlist if you already have a date.

01

You rent the bar

You rent the finished 1963 Yellowstone trailer — hatch, ice, lighting, and optional glassware. It arrives with the 3600.

02

You bring the pour

You supply beverages, mixers, and bartenders. If the venue requires a Virginia ABC banquet license, the host obtains it. Cork & Carriage does not sell alcohol.

03

We bring the pair

We deliver the truck and the bar, set them up, power them, and collect them after. One pair, one date.
